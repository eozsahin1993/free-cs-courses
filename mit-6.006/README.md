# MIT 6.006 — Introduction to Algorithms

## Links

- Course site (OCW): https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-006-introduction-to-algorithms-fall-2011/
- Textbook: Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein, *Introduction to Algorithms* (CLRS), 3rd ed., MIT Press, 2009 — required, with a per-lecture chapter/section reading listed in each week file.
- YouTube playlist: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUl4u3cNGP61Oq3tWYp6V_F-5jb5L2iHb — every lecture and recitation video linked in the week files is from this playlist (per-video IDs individually confirmed via YouTube's oEmbed API, not scraped from the page's dynamic JS).

## Why Fall 2011, not a newer offering

6.006 has a newer OCW offering (Spring 2020, also Demaine) with more problem sets and quizzes.
Fall 2011 was chosen instead because it has the more complete package for self-study: **23 of 24
recitations have their own video** (Spring 2020 has recitation *notes* but no recitation video at
all, and its separate "Problem Session" series is only recorded for 5 of 9 sessions), and every
lecture has an explicit CLRS chapter/section mapping (Spring 2020 treats CLRS as optional/not
required, so no such mapping exists). All lecture, recitation, problem-set, and exam links below
were individually verified to resolve — none are broken or behind a login.

## Structure

- [weeks/](weeks/) — one file per week (`week-01.md` … `week-12.md`), each self-contained: that
  week's two lectures, the recitations that pair with them, problem sets (assigned/due), and
  exams. Calendar dates are stripped out — self-paced, not tied to a semester.

Recitations are supplementary problem-walkthrough sessions, not separate lecture content — treat
them as optional but genuinely useful (they're where the TAs work practice problems live).
Recitations 4 and 17 were never recorded and don't exist anywhere; not a broken link.
